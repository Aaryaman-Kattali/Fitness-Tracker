# 12-Week Foundation Programme
**Goal:** lean muscular build + plyometric athleticism | **Start:** 66.65 kg, 5'10", 22

---

## THE WEEK

| Day | Session | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Rest — Daily 15 only (the gym is shut) | 15 min |
| Tue | Lower A — Quad/Glute + Plyo | 70 min |
| Wed | Upper A — Push emphasis | 65 min |
| Thu | Cardio + Daily 15 | 35 min |
| Fri | Lower B — Hinge/Posterior + Plyo | 70 min |
| Sat | Upper B — Pull emphasis | 65 min |
| Sun | Cardio + Daily 15 | 35 min |

The gym closes on Mondays, so the week is shifted one day and the old
Sunday walk/mobility slot becomes the second cardio session. **Session
content is unchanged** — only which day each one falls on moved.

**Daily 15** (home mobility + core) happens every single day, including gym days. It is the part that fixes your posture. Do not skip it.

---

## PHASE 1 — WEEKS 1–8

### TUESDAY — Lower A

**Warm-up (8 min)**
- Skipping rope, easy pace — 3 × 1 min
- Leg swings front/back and side/side — 10 each
- Bodyweight glute bridge — 2 × 15
- Bodyweight squat — 2 × 10, slow

**Plyometrics (8 min, before lifting, fresh)**

*Weeks 1–4:*
- Pogo hops (stiff ankles, minimal knee bend, bounce off the floor) — 4 × 20
- Drop-and-stick from a low bench/step (~30 cm) — 3 × 5, land soft, hold 2 sec
- Line hops, forward-back and side-side — 3 × 20 sec each

*Weeks 5–8:*
- Pogo hops — 3 × 20
- Broad jump, stick the landing — 4 × 3 (full rest between reps)
- Lateral bound, stick each landing — 3 × 5/side

**Lift**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Smith machine squat | 4 × 6–8 | Feet slightly forward of the bar. Full depth. |
| DB Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 8–10/leg | Torso slightly forward — this loads the glute. |
| Smith machine hip thrust | 3 × 10–12 | Pad the bar. Chin tucked, ribs down, squeeze 1 sec at top. |
| Leg extension | 3 × 12–15 | |
| Standing calf raise | 4 × 10–12 | 3-second lower on every rep. |
| Tibialis raise (heels on floor, back to wall, lift toes) | 3 × 15–20 | Your shin armour for running. |

### WEDNESDAY — Upper A (Push emphasis)

**Warm-up (5 min)**
- Band pull-aparts — 2 × 20
- Thoracic extension over a rolled towel — 10 slow reps
- Doorway pec stretch — 30 sec/side
- Cable face pull, very light — 2 × 15

**Lift**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seated incline chest press | 4 × 8–10 | Squeeze the handles hard. Full-body tension. |
| Lat pulldown | 4 × 8–12 | Pull to upper chest, elbows down not back. |
| Seated DB shoulder press | 3 × 8–12 | Start at 7.5 kg. Ribs down, don't arch. |
| Cable row (neutral handle) | 3 × 10–12 | Pause 1 sec with shoulder blades pinched. |
| Cable lateral raise | 3 × 12–15 | Light. This is a small muscle. |
| Face pull, high to eye level | 3 × 15–20 | External rotation at the end. Posture money. |
| Triceps rope pushdown | 3 × 12–15 | |

### FRIDAY — Lower B

**Warm-up (8 min)**
- Skipping rope — 3 × 1 min
- 90/90 hip switches — 10/side
- Band clamshells — 2 × 15/side
- Bodyweight RDL, slow — 2 × 10

**Plyometrics (8 min)**

*Weeks 1–4:*
- Skipping, single-leg alternating — 3 × 30 sec
- Ankle hops in place, fast and stiff — 4 × 15
- Split-stance jump (scissor), soft landings — 3 × 6

*Weeks 5–8:*
- Low box jump (~40 cm), step down — 4 × 4
- Bounding for distance — 3 × 20 m
- Single-leg hop and stick — 3 × 4/side

**Lift**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Smith machine RDL | 4 × 8–10 | Bar close to legs, push hips back, feel the hamstrings. |
| Seated leg press | 4 × 10–12 | Feet mid-platform, don't let the lower back round. |
| Prone leg curl | 3 × 12–15 | 3-second lower. |
| DB step-up onto bench | 3 × 10/leg | Drive through the heel, no push off the back foot. |
| Seated calf raise (or standing) | 4 × 12–15 | |
| Band clamshell + side-lying abduction | 3 × 12/side | Glute medius. Fixes hip stability for running. |

## THE DAILY 15 (at home, every day)

**Morning — 5 min**
1. Thoracic extension over rolled towel — 10 slow reps
2. Chin tucks — 15 reps (hold 3 sec each)
3. Band pull-aparts — 20 reps
4. Doorway pec stretch — 30 sec/side

**Evening — 10 min**

*Core (anti-extension — this is what fixes APT):*
1. Dead bug — 3 × 8/side, slow, lower back pressed flat into the floor
2. Side plank — 3 × 30 sec/side
3. Hollow body hold — 3 × 20 sec
4. Bird dog — 2 × 8/side

*Stretch:*
5. Kneeling hip flexor stretch (squeeze the glute of the back leg — critical) — 45 sec/side
6. 90/90 hip stretch — 45 sec/side
7. Standing calf + soleus stretch against wall — 30 sec each/side
8. Glute bridge hold with band around knees — 3 × 20 sec

> **On the hip flexor stretch:** if you don't squeeze the back-leg glute, you're stretching the ligament, not the muscle, and nothing changes. The glute squeeze is the whole exercise.

## CARDIO (Thu + Sun, while the rain is on)

Pick one, 25–30 min:
- **Skipping rope intervals** — 1 min on / 1 min off × 12. Best option: builds calf and tibialis stiffness, trains exactly the elasticity you want, and works in a small indoor space.
- **Stair climbing** — 20 min continuous, easy pace
- **Bodyweight circuit** — 25 min easy, non-competing with lifting

Plus **8,000+ steps/day**.

---

## RETURN TO RUNNING (when the rain eases)

You were at 13 km peak and 61-min 10k, so you have a base — but 4+ weeks off means you rebuild, not resume. Replace the Thu/Sun cardio slots.

| Week | Sessions | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 20 min easy, conversational |
| 2 | 2 | 25 min easy |
| 3 | 3 | 25 / 30 / 25 min |
| 4 | 3 | 30 / 35 / 30 min |
| 5 | 3 | 30 / 40 / 30 min |
| 6+ | 3 | +10% total weekly volume, max |

**Rules:**
- All easy pace for the first 4 weeks. No time trials. Your 10k time will come back fast and be faster than before.
- Never run the day before Lower A or Lower B.
- Once running 3×/week, drop the plyometrics to Lower A only — running is itself plyometric loading.
- If shin, knee, or hip pain shows up, hold the current volume for a week rather than pushing through.

---

## HOW TO PROGRESS

**Double progression.** Hit the top of the rep range on *every* set for two sessions running → add weight (2.5 kg on machines/Smith, one dumbbell step) and drop back to the bottom of the range.

**Where to start:** pick a weight you could do 3–4 more reps with than the target. It will feel too easy in week 1. That's correct — week 1 is skill practice, not a test.

**Effort:** stop 1–2 reps short of failure on compounds, 0–1 short on isolation. Never grind a rep that stalls.

**Rest:** 3 min on the first two exercises of each day, 90 sec on the rest.

**Log everything.** Weight, sets, reps, every session. This is non-negotiable — progression is invisible without it.

---

## FORCE PRODUCTION CHECKLIST (before every heavy set)

1. Grip the handle/bar as hard as you can
2. Big breath into the belly, brace 360° like taking a punch
3. Squeeze glutes, drive feet into the floor
4. Ramped warm-up sets — never go cold into the working weight

Worth 10–20% immediately. Most beginners lift with a loose grip and a soft body.

## NUTRITION (recap)

- **~2,650 kcal/day** at maintenance — verify by tracking 2 weeks and watching the scale
- **Protein 140–150 g**, split into 4 meals of ~35–40 g
- **Fat 60–70 g**
- **Carbs ~370 g**
- **Creatine monohydrate 5 g/day**, any time, no loading phase
- Sleep 7–9 hrs

---

## WHAT TO TRACK

**Weekly:** bodyweight (daily, use the 7-day average), waist at navel, all lift numbers.

**Monthly:** photos — front, side, back, same light, same time of day, relaxed.

**Every 4 weeks:** standing broad jump distance. It's the cleanest single measure of the athleticism you're chasing.

---

## MILESTONES

**Week 4** — Lifts up 15–25%. Posture already visibly different. Weight roughly flat.

**Week 8** — Bulgarian split squats with 12.5–15 kg DBs. Overhead pressing 12.5 kg per hand. Broad jump up 15–20 cm. Waist down 1–2 cm.

**Week 12** — ~+2 kg lean mass. Smith squat around bodyweight. Reassess and move to Phase 2 (heavier compounds, real plyometrics, running volume back up).

---

## PHASE 2 PREVIEW (Week 13+)

Rep ranges drop to 5–8 on main lifts. Plyometrics progress to depth drops and reactive work. Running volume returns toward 13 km+. Calories go to a slight surplus (~2,900) if the mirror has stalled.
